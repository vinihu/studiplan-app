Summary: Cell division

Summary · Short · Made 2 Oct 2026 with Claude Code (sonnet)

The demo opens this same saved summary whatever length you chose, and shows its first part.

This material covers cell division: the cell cycle, the four phases of mitosis, how the cycle is controlled, and mitosis compared with meiosis.

The cell cycle

  • Interphase is the longest part of the cycle:
    • G1: the cell grows and makes proteins and organelles.
    • S: the DNA is replicated. Each chromosome then consists of two sister chromatids joined at the centromere.
    • G2: the cell grows further and prepares for division.
  • The M phase is mitosis (division of the nucleus) plus cytokinesis (division of the cytoplasm).
  • Cells divide for growth, for repair and replacement, and in some organisms for asexual reproduction.
  • A large cell has a problem: its volume grows faster than its surface area, so exchange across the membrane becomes too slow.

A cell becomes very large. Why does this cause a problem?

  • Its volume grows faster than its surface area, so exchange across the membrane becomes too slowCorrect answer
  • Its surface area grows faster than its volume, so too many materials enter at onceYour answer
  • Its DNA can no longer be replicated during S phase
  • Its nuclear envelope can no longer break down in prophase
Why

Volume increases faster than surface area, so the membrane cannot exchange materials fast enough. This is one reason cells divide. The other options describe things the material does not link to cell size.

Installing on Windows

It installs for you alone, without administrator rights. Uninstalling removes the program and leaves your library and files alone.

The installer is not signed: a code-signing certificate costs money, and this free project has none. So Windows SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”. Two clicks get you past it:

  1. 1More info
  3. 2Run anyway

On a Windows 11 PC with Smart App Control turned on, Windows will not let the installer run at all.

Check your download

Because the installer is not signed, here is its SHA-256. In PowerShell, in the folder you downloaded it to, run:

Get-FileHash .\Studiplan-Setup-0.1.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256

The result must be exactly this:

SHA256-OF-THE-RELEASED-INSTALLER
