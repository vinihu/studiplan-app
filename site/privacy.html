Privacy

Studiplan has no account, no tracking and no server of its own. This page says plainly what the app and this website do with your data.

The app

  • Nothing goes to the makers of Studiplan. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics, and there is no Studiplan server to send anything to.
  • Your files stay on your computer. Your materials, the results made from them and your settings are ordinary files in a folder you choose.
  • When you press a Make button, your material goes to the AI you chose. Its text and photos are sent to Claude Code, Codex, the provider of your API key (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), or Ollama on your own computer. What happens to it there is governed by that company’s own terms and privacy policy, not by Studiplan.
  • API keys are stored encrypted on your computer by the operating system, and are sent only to the provider they belong to.
  • No other connections. As of this release the app’s window is blocked from all web requests. The only connections going out are the AI requests you start, and whatever the AI tools you have installed do themselves.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, and no scripts from other websites.
  • It is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes visitors’ IP addresses and request logs in order to deliver the page. See Vercel’s privacy policy.
  • The download is served by GitHub. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • The Discord link leads to Discord, which has its own privacy policy.

Students and children

Nothing is collected, so there is nothing to consent to. There is no sign-up.

Contact

Questions about this page: open an issue on the project’s GitHub issues page, or ask on the Discord server.
